How the Vernier scale works

Physics notes
Least count = 1 main-scale division − 1 Vernier-scale division
In this simulation: 1 MSD = 1 mm and 20 VSD = 19 mm, so 1 VSD = 0.95 mm and LC = 1.00 − 0.95 = 0.05 mm.
Final reading = MSR + (VC × LC) − zero error
VC is the index (0–19) of the coinciding Vernier division. If zero error is negative, subtracting it adds the magnitude of the error.
Important calibration note: “10 Vernier divisions = 9 mm” gives a least count of 0.10 mm when 1 MSD = 1 mm—not 0.05 mm. To teach 0.05 mm correctly, this lab uses 20 Vernier divisions = 19 mm in the calculation and in the inset schematic. The uploaded GLB's engraved Vernier markings are part of its original texture and may not visually match this pedagogical inset exactly.
